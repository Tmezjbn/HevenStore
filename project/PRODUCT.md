# Product

**Purpose:** Brand, users, and design principles for HEVEN.FUN — what we are building and what we refuse.

**Read this when:** starting UI/UX work, writing storefront copy, or choosing a visual direction. Pair with `DESIGN.md`.

## Register

product

## Users

- **Primary:** Global gamers shopping for digital games, subscriptions, gift cards, and one-time keys. Arabic and English are equally important — users switch languages and expect RTL/LTR to feel native, not mirrored as an afterthought.
- **Secondary:** Store staff (`owner` / `admin` / `moderator` / `support`) and sellers running catalog, orders, support desk, and storefront settings via the dashboard. Role duties: `HANDOFF.md`.

## Product Purpose

HEVEN.FUN is a digital entertainment storefront: browse, compare deals, purchase, and receive keys or subscription access quickly. Success means shoppers trust the store enough to complete checkout on first visit, and staff can run the business without leaving a polished, coherent product surface.

## Brand Personality

Premium, fast, trustworthy. A dark gaming boutique — confident and sharp, not loud or discount-bin. Speed and clarity signal competence; the default Vault skin is calm monochrome, with user-selectable skins (Aurora, Sunset, Abyss, Dim, Coffee) carrying energy without carnival hype.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS marketing patterns: cream/sand backgrounds, gradient text, hero metric templates, uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section, identical icon-card grids, and decorative motion that delays the purchase path.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust before flash** — delivery speed, secure payment, and support visibility outweigh visual spectacle on every commerce surface.
2. **Speed is the product** — browsing, cart, checkout, and post-purchase flows should feel immediate; motion supports orientation, never blocks action.
3. **Bilingual by default** — AR/EN parity in copy, layout, and component behavior; RTL is a first-class layout mode, not a CSS patch.
4. **Commerce clarity over decoration** — price, discount, stock, and primary CTA stay visually dominant; ornament earns its pixels.
5. **Admin matches storefront quality** — dashboard tools should feel like the same brand, not a separate cheap panel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Baseline bar: fix obvious contrast, focus, and keyboard issues as surfaces are touched. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` on new motion work. No formal WCAG certification target yet; elevate to AA when shipping public-facing flows to production.
